## Cross-cutting observations (not new content, just noting recurring structure)

- All six examples use the identical 5-step template: (1) trend stalls into a range, (2) reaction at a range extreme forms a double top/bottom, (3) directional bias stated, (4) Volume Profile VAH/VAL/LVN marked, (5) Footprint/Delta candle-by-candle read at the Spring/Upthrust location — `[text]`, observed across p281–p320.
- The recurring qualitative framework applied to each numbered footprint candle is: **Volume** (khối lượng giao dịch), **Delta**, **Giá** (price behavior relative to the Trading Range and to Stacked Imbalances), and **Kết luận** (a verdict framed around "sự hài hòa giữa nỗ lực và kết quả" — harmony/mismatch between effort and result) — this effort-vs-result framing appears on every single worked example page in this range and is the book's operative "Tape Reading Bar by Bar" method for this chapter.
- Every Spring example (GMX, CHZ, NFP, ETHW, RDNT) is classified by the author as "**Spring tiềm năng loại 2**" (a "Type 2 potential Spring") — the specific defining criteria for "loại 2" are given inline each time (moderate-volume break of support, quick reclaim into the range, one or more retests of the broken support) but a formal enumerated Type-1/Type-2/etc. taxonomy is not shown on any page in this range — if such a taxonomy exists it must be defined elsewhere in the book, outside files 281–320.
- Every long example uses the same trade-management language: Stop Loss below the lowest of the two bottoms; Take Profit initially at the top of the Trading Range, then trail stop to breakeven as price approaches it, exiting on a marked reversal candle (often flagged by an arrow) showing a Stacked Imbalance against the trade direction, with a stated realized RR (risk/reward) value per trade (GMX: 4.10; CHZ: 2.59; NFP: 1.88; ETHW: 4.06; RDNT: 1.74).
