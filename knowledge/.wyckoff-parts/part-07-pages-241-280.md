## 2. End of prior chapter: limits of structure-only analysis (p241–p242)

- `[text]` (p241, printed folio "242") — continuation of a critique of doing Wyckoff structural analysis without going deeper into identifying key price levels where supply/demand interact strongly; doing so "can lead to skipping over areas that could create optimal entry or exit points." Two further named shortcomings (bulleted in the book):
  - **"Thiếu thông tin về khối lượng tập trung"** (Lack of information about concentrated volume): volume analysis and price-action analysis give an overview but no detail on how volume is distributed at specific price levels — important for understanding how the market reacts at specific price points.
  - **"Thiếu chiến lược giao dịch cụ thể"** (Lack of a specific trading strategy): structural analysis provides necessary knowledge about the market but does not address setting up a concrete trading strategy based on identified price levels, limiting the ability to capture opportunities.
  - Closing line: "Vì những thiếu sót trên mà bạn đọc cần tiếp tục nghiên cứu các chương sau của quyển sách này." (Because of these shortcomings, the reader needs to continue studying the following chapters.)

## 3. Chapter 9 — "Phân tích vùng" (Zone Analysis): Volume Profile + Wyckoff (p243–p249)

- `[text]` Chapter title page (p243): **"CHƯƠNG 9 — PHÂN TÍCH VÙNG"**. Intro paragraph: after determining market context and buy/sell bias, the next important step is identifying the price zone/location where price is expected to develop, to trigger order entry. The chapter teaches identifying potential price zones to wait for entry using the **Volume Profile** tool combined with Wyckoff structure, addressing the limitations of structural analysis alone discussed in the previous chapter.
- `[text]` **"Bước 4: Phân tích vùng"** (Step 4: Zone Analysis) (p243): In Wyckoff structure, the Trading Range is typically identified as an accumulation or distribution zone where price oscillates within a set range. When using Volume Profile, the **Value Area (VAH and VAL)** often reflects these ranges. Specifically, VAH and VAL mark the zone where **68.2%** of trading volume occurred and typically lie near or coincide with the extremes of the Trading Range.
- `[text]` (p244, printed folio "245") — continuing: these are key points to observe potential price reversals. Newer/less experienced traders can use this tool to more accurately identify Trading Ranges matching the Wyckoff pattern. Summary bullet: **"Xác định VAL, VAH – Các mức giá chờ tiềm năng."** (Identify VAL, VAH — potential price levels to wait for.) VAH and VAL mark areas where the market previously showed significant trading volume; these zones are likely to cause price reversal when price returns to them, because traders tend to look back at these price levels as reference points for buy/sell decisions. When price approaches **VAL**, a trader may view this as a potential price to look for a buying opportunity (**looking for a Spring**) because it is a price the market previously found attractive to buy. Conversely, when price approaches **VAH**, a trader may view this as a potential price to look for a selling opportunity (**looking for an Upthrust**) because it is a level that previously met strong market resistance. When a Spring or Upthrust per Wyckoff structure occurs near the VAH/VAL zones per Volume Profile, these zones can provide additional evidence and confidence in a potential price reversal.
- `[text]` (p245, printed folio "246") — note: VAH and VAL are price levels (default computed by software at the 68.2% ratio); traders should not treat them as rigid levels but as potential zones. Typically, below VAL and above VAH is an area of low trading volume, called **LVN (Low Volume Node)** — this is where price reactions at VAH or VAL continue to play out. Bullet: **"Xác định vùng giá LVN – để tiếp tục theo dõi các phản ứng giá và dự kiến khu vực cắt lỗ."** (Identify the LVN price zone — to continue monitoring price reactions and to anticipate the stop-loss area.) LVN is typically where price-rejection reactions occur; when price approaches this zone, the probability of rejection is high, so LVN is often seen as an ideal place to set a stop-loss, protecting the trader's position. However, note: when price crosses the VAH or VAL thresholds and moves into the LVN zone, this can signal a potential shift in market dynamics. If reversal signals at LVN are unclear or do not appear, it means the market is trending and price may continue moving outside the VAL zone. In that situation, plans for a potential Spring or Upthrust are no longer valid; price may defy the original plan and continue its trend. Therefore, continued analysis of signals at VAH, VAL, and LVN is necessary — one should not stop here and assume price will reverse at these zones. (Book text says deeper analysis is presented "in Chapter 9" even though this passage is itself within Chapter 9 per the p243 title — transcribed verbatim, not corrected.)

## 4. Chapter 10 — "Phân tích tín hiệu vào lệnh" (Entry Signal Analysis): Footprint, Delta, Tape Reading (p250–p267)

- `[text]` Chapter title page (p250): **"CHƯƠNG 10 — PHÂN TÍCH TÍN HIỆU VÀO LỆNH"**. Intro: after determining market context (Chapter 8) and key price levels via Volume Profile (Chapter 9), the chapter explores how traders analyze signals to make actual trading decisions. Chapter 10 goes deep into using the **Footprint chart** and **Delta analysis** to decode market signals such as imbalance, absorption, and active participation by large institutions, to find suitable entry points. States: "Đây là lúc bạn cần sử dụng Footprint, Delta, Delta tích lũy như một chiếc kính lúp để quan sát." (This is when you need to use Footprint, Delta, and cumulative Delta like a magnifying glass to observe.) Combined with **Tape Reading** technique to better understand market context and the dynamics behind price moves. Notes the chapter is not just theory but "the art of adjusting strategy based on live market data."
- `[text]` (p251, printed folio "251") — **"Bước 5: Phân tích sự kiệt sức, hấp thụ và phát triển"** (Step 5: Analyze exhaustion, absorption, and development). Sub-step **a. Phân tích sự kiệt sức (nếu có)** (Analyze exhaustion, if present): "Sự kiệt sức là dấu hiệu đầu tiên cho thấy một xu hướng có thể sắp kết thúc." (Exhaustion is the first sign that a trend may be about to end.)
  - **Đối với Spring** (For a Spring), exhaustion signs:
    - **Sự sụt giảm khối lượng** (Volume decline): as price approaches/breaks a key support level in the Trading Range, watch volume. A volume decline as price touches the low shows the selling side losing strength — usually a sign there isn't much more selling pressure. Observable via a normal volume indicator or via the **Footprint Chart**.
    - **Delta và Delta tích lũy** (Delta and cumulative Delta): Delta turns positive (green/xanh) or less negative when the sell side is fully exhausted. Cumulative Delta rises gradually and can shift from negative to positive, showing the sell side no longer has the capacity to resist rising buying pressure.
- `[text]` (p252) — continued **Spring exhaustion**: **Phản ứng giá** (Price reaction): because there is no longer enough selling pressure to push price lower, a temporary bottom or a double-bottom pattern ("mô hình hai đáy") may appear. Another price reaction showing exhaustion is **SOT** (Sign of Test? — book gives no expansion of the acronym on this page): "SOT thể hiện một sự không tương xứng giữa nỗ lực và kết quả trong phân tích thị trường." (SOT reflects a mismatch between effort and result in market analysis.) Specifically: down-moves become progressively shorter and fail to reach new lows, even though volume may not decline proportionally — showing that strong selling effort is no longer effective, signaling the sell side is running out of steam and a bottom may be near.
  - **Đối với Upthrust** (For an Upthrust), exhaustion signs:
    - **Sự thay đổi của khối lượng** (Volume change): as price approaches and breaks a resistance level, a notable volume increase is typical. However, to confirm an Upthrust, the trader wants to see high volume as price breaks resistance but then a sharp volume decline as price falls back below resistance — showing the buy side's participation wasn't strong enough to sustain a higher price. Observable via normal volume indicator or Footprint Chart.
    - **Delta và Delta tích lũy**: large positive Delta (green) as price breaks resistance can quickly flip to negative (red) as price reverses.
- `[text]` (p253) — continued: cumulative Delta gradually shifts from positive to negative, showing the buy side is no longer in control and the sell side is gaining the upper hand. **Phản ứng giá tiêu cực** (Negative price reaction): with insufficient buying pressure to push price higher, a temporary top or double-top pattern ("mô hình hai đỉnh") may appear. Another reaction showing buy-side exhaustion is **SOT**: in the approach to an Upthrust, up-moves can get progressively shorter, showing the buy side losing strength; the shrinking size of up-moves before/after breaking resistance is a sign buying pressure is weakening.
  - Note: "trong một số trường hợp, dấu hiệu kiệt sức có thể sẽ không xuất hiện, lúc này Trader chỉ cần bỏ qua bước này và tiếp tục phân tích các bước tiếp theo." (In some cases the exhaustion signal may not appear; the trader can simply skip this step and continue to the next steps.)
  - **b. Phân tích sự hấp thụ** (Absorption analysis): defined as observing and recognizing large traders stopping the current trend.
    - **Đối với Spring**: **Khối lượng tăng mạnh mẽ** (Volume rises sharply): for absorption, the buy side doesn't just wait for the sell side to exhaust but actively "absorbs" ("nuốt" — literally "swallows") the volume of selling. Typically occurs when volume spikes at a support level — a sign the buy side is actively absorbing sell-side supply.
- `[text]` (p254, page rotated) — continued: Footprint chart records unusual volume concentrated at certain price levels of the Footprint candle in the **BID** column. This repeated imbalance phenomenon creates **stacked imbalances**, usually displayed by software as a colored line/band on the chart.
  - **Delta và Delta tích lũy**: Delta starts negative as price falls, but the sell side fails to keep pushing price down through the support level. Delta afterward shifts to positive as the buy side begins absorbing sell pressure in an effective manner, marking a change of control to the buy side. Cumulative Delta rises steadily and can flip from negative to positive, showing the sell side's growing loss of control and the buy side's strengthening grip.
  - Second bullet on absorption at Spring: **Giá không di chuyển sau khi khối lượng lớn xuất hiện** (Price does not move after large volume appears): if after large volume appears price does not continue moving, and this is because large limit sell orders are blocking the price from moving higher, this normally shows up as large candles/hurdles absorbing the buy pressure and preparing to push price up.
- `[text]` (p255) — **Đối với Upthrust** absorption:
  - **Khối lượng tăng mạnh mẽ**: volume spikes sharply as price breaks resistance, potentially showing the buy side trying but failing to gain control. Volume spike observable via normal volume indicator or Footprint Chart. Footprint chart records unusual volume concentrated at certain price levels in the **ASK** column. Repeated imbalances create stacked imbalances, usually displayed with a blue/color line on the chart.
  - **Delta và Delta tích lũy**: as price starts breaking resistance, Delta is initially positive (buy effort), but this doesn't last long — as the sell side begins absorbing buy pressure, Delta turns negative, showing that despite buying effort, the sell side successfully absorbed and neutralized it. For cumulative Delta: while the buy side is still trying to gain control, cumulative Delta may rise slightly (reflecting buy effort to break resistance); however once the sell side begins absorbing buy volume and gaining control—
- `[text]` (p256) — continued: cumulative Delta begins declining, shifting from positive to negative, showing the buy side weakening and the sell side strengthening.
  - **Giá không di chuyển sau khi khối lượng lớn xuất hiện** (Price does not move after large volume): if after large volume appears price does not continue rising but instead falls back, this shows large limit sell orders blocked price from moving higher; typically large candles or sequences of large candles appear, showing that sell-side effort successfully absorbed the buy volume and is preparing to push price lower.
  - **c. Phân tích sự phát triển** (Development analysis): after establishing absorption, the next step is looking for **development**, an important factor confirming trend reversal. Development is defined as the active action of large traders pushing price beyond support/resistance levels, creating a new trend.
    - **Đối với Spring**: **Khối lượng tăng đột biến và duy trì ở mức cao** (Volume spikes and stays elevated): confirms the buy side continues to control the market. Footprint Chart records unusual volume concentrated at certain price levels in the **ASK** column (unlike the BID column for absorption).
- `[text]` (p257) — repeated line: stacked imbalances shown by software with colored lines. **Delta và Delta tích lũy** for development: Delta continues to shift from negative to positive, reflecting the change of control from sell to buy side. Cumulative Delta continues rising, showing continued buy-side momentum.
  - **Giá tăng mạnh** (Price rises strongly): the market shows one or more **SOS (Sign of Strength)** candles — large candles with long bodies closing higher than they open, showing the buy side not only pushed price up but also sustained buying pressure.
  - **Đối với Upthrust**: **Khối lượng vẫn cao ngay cả khi giá bắt đầu giảm** (Volume stays high even as price starts to fall): when price breaks resistance, volume typically spikes. However, when price turns down and volume remains persistently high, this reinforces confidence in sell-side control. Footprint Chart records unusual volume concentrated in the **BID** column (unlike absorption, where large volume concentrated in ASK). Repeated imbalances create stacked imbalances recorded by red lines on the chart.
- `[text]` (p258) — **Delta và Delta tích lũy** (Upthrust development): when Upthrust occurs and sell side begins to control, Delta can flip negative (red), reflecting sell-side control. Cumulative Delta continues declining after peaking, confirming sell-side control of the market.
  - **Giá giảm mạnh** (Price falls sharply): market shows one or more consecutive **SOW (Sign of Weakness)** candles — large candles with long bodies closing low relative to open, showing strong sell-side participation; price may close below resistance.
  - **d. Tổng hợp và xác định điểm vào lệnh** (Synthesis and determining entry point):
    - **Tổng hợp dữ liệu** (Synthesize data) — Mục tiêu (Goal): combine all information gathered to determine the type of Spring or Upthrust to make an accurate decision. Thực hiện (Execution): use the checklist below to synthesize and determine the entry point per the figure below or Appendix 01 (Phụ lục 01) attached.
