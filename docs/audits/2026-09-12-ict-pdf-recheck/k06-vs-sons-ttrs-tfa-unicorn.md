# Audit: `knowledge/06-ttrades-models.md` vs the five slide decks

Scope: `Sons_Model.pdf` (6 pp), `Sons_Model_HTF.pdf` (9 pp), `TTrades Reversal Sequence (TTRS).pdf` (18 pp), `Timeframe Alignement TTrades.pdf` (7 pp), `Unicorn_Model.pdf` (7 pp). Every page was viewed as a 70 dpi PNG; five pages (TFA p3, TTRS p6, p10, p17, Sons p5) were additionally re-rendered at 200 dpi and cropped to settle candle-level questions (`scratchpad/zoom/`). Page numbers below are physical; printed slide number in parentheses.

Finding types: MISSING = deck content absent from the file; WRONG = file contradicts the page; UNSOURCED = file asserts deck content no page shows; AMBIGUOUS = diagram admits more than one reading and the file picked one silently. Severity: high = would change a trading rule / level / number; low = cosmetic or wording.

## Verified as stated (no discrepancy)

- **Sons Model / Sons HTF triplets** — p3–p5 (1–3): "H1 or M15 Chart" → "M5 Chart" → "30s Chart" ("M5 Stop Raid" label). HTF p3–p5: "H4 or H1" → "M15" → "1m" ("M15 Stop Raid"). HTF p6–p8: "D1 or H4" → "H1" → "m5" ("H1 Stop Raid"). Drawings identical across all three scales. §2.10, §5 table row correct.
- **Sons "Stop Raid" picture** (p4 / HTF p4, p7): "Draw On Liquidity" line above; a prior M5 low (solid then dotted line labelled "Stop Raid"); a yellow-highlighted **green** candle opens above the low, wicks below it, closes above; rally follows. §2.10 / §3.3 rule 2 correct.
- **Sons "Entry" picture** (p5 / HTF p5, p8): displacement up leaves an FVG; retrace; expansion. No entry marker, no stop, no target drawn anywhere in either deck. §3.3 rule 4 / §6 item 6 correct.
- **TTRS Killzones** p3 (1): Asia 20:00–00:00, London 02:00–05:00, NY AM – Forex 07:00–10:00, NY AM – Indices 08:30–11:00, London Close – Forex 10:00–12:00, NY PM – Indices 13:30–16:00, caption "Eastern Standard Time (EST)". §2.9 and §5 exact.
- **TTRS Timeframes** p4 (2): M15 (Patient), M1 (Impatient); two bullet sentences quoted correctly (deck typo "suites" silently corrected).
- **TTRS FVG** p5 (3): candles numbered 1-2-3; bearish gap = candle-1 low / candle-3 high; bullish = candle-1 high / candle-3 low. Correct.
- **TTRS Inversion** p6 (4): bearish FVG on the way down; green candle closes through it; a later green candle's lower wick dips back into the inverted gap and holds (200 dpi `zoom/ttrs6-inv.png`). "retested from above as support" is supported.
- **TTRS OB** p7 (5): blue OB line at the open of the last opposing-colour candle (lowest up-close / highest down-close), close through it. Correct (see finding 5 for the missing qualifier).

- **TTRS Premium/Discount** p11 (9): blue OB/CISD line at the open of the lowest up-close candle of the two-candle series, in the premium half; price falls into discount. Correct.
- **TTRS SMT** p12 (10): "Stronger Asset" higher low vs "Weaker Asset" lower low, orange divergence lines, both rally. Correct.
- **TTRS Order of reversal** p13–p17 (11–15): labels appear one per slide in the exact order "1. turtle soup", "2. inversion", "3. cisd / ob", "4. fvg", "5. breaker". Turtle soup = pink box under the dotted prior low; inversion = bearish FVG formed by the two down-close candles into the sweep, closed through by the sweep candle; CISD = blue line at the open of the **highest** down-close candle of the final run, closed above two candles later; FVG = thin yellow box from the candle that closed above the CISD line, sitting just above the blue line; pullback then expansion. No stop or target on any slide. §2.9 / §3.4 order correct.
