## Reading

1. **What survived is small.** +1.9 bp per trade on gold at ~2 trades a day is a thin edge: as a fraction of the 2-hour move
   (excess z 0.068) it is roughly 0.05-0.10 R per trade for a stop near one standard deviation of that move. By the
   barrier simulation in docs/audits/2026-10-02-strategic-diagnosis.md §4.3, that is in the range where FTMO Phase 1 can be
   passed with a decent probability only at ~2-3 trades a day and ~0.5 % risk -- i.e. as part of a book, not alone.
2. **What failed is informative.** The census's strongest gold signal (E1, prior-day sweep -> continuation) weakened to
   insignificance on 2024-26 (net still positive, p 0.06-0.23); E2 (fade on gold) REVERSED sign; nothing on DE40 or USTEC
   passes. The census showed regime-dependent effects, which is exactly what this stage exists to discard.
3. **The FVG retrace is the one ICT primitive with evidence in all three reads** (census discovery, census confirmation,
   exposed window): continuation after a retrace into a displacement gap. It is a MECHANICAL primitive with a time exit;
   the full ICT setup chain built around it never showed a net edge (diagnosis §3).
