## 1. Bottom line (read this first)

Per candidate, with the PRIMARY model (measured pooled trade rate, regime correlation 0.3, cost 0.07R). `e*` = the net edge at which
`prop_pass_probability` first reaches 0.70 on the binding fund; `e_min` = the earlier rule's floor-bound-only 80 % power edge;
`e80` = the smallest edge on the 0.05 grid at which the WHOLE sealed conjunction passes with probability >= 0.80.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 5m-metals Wyckoff | 0.104 | 0.175 | **not reached** (P <= 0.78 up to 1.0) | n/a | 0.00 | 0/3000 | **frequency** (22-29 %), then shifted prop pass |

1. **The sealed procedure can certify, with 80 % power, only an edge of 1.45-1.8 x the prop-relevant edge e* (1m-metals, 1m-indices).**
   At a true edge exactly equal to e* it passes 0-10 % of the time; the expected number of the six candidates that pass if each had its own
   e* is **0.27**. The floor bound alone would have detected 0.10-0.20 R; the other checks cost another 0.15-0.25 R of detectable edge (1m cells).
2. **The check that costs the power is the shifted prop pass (A8b).** It re-runs the prop pass with every R reduced by (mean - primary
   bound), i.e. on a stream whose mean IS the floor bound; so it asks that the bound itself, not the mean, reach e*. At e80/2 it fails in
   100 % of the replications; at e80 it is still the most frequent failure of the 1m cells (11-12 %). Stress (p90 spread plus the commission margin
   costs 0.13-0.18 R on the metals, 0.04-0.07 R on the indices in this model) is the second cost at low edges (65 % and 99 % of the 1m-metals replications at e80/2).
3. **5m-metals cannot be certified with 80 % power at any edge up to 1.0 under the primary model.** The frequency rule (every one of the 7 folds must have all entry
   gaps <= 30 days: `plan --dry-run` prints "7 of 7 folds, 0 may fail") fails in 22-41 % of replications regardless of the edge, because at
   ~155-170 pooled trades a year with month-level rate dispersion sigma 0.75 a 30-day hole in at least one of seven years is common. This
   is a property of the rule and the trade rate, not of the edge. Under the earlier rule's conservative rate convention it almost never
   passes (P <= 0.13). It depends on the dispersion assumption (sigma 0.5 lowers the ICT e80 to <= 0.40), which is NOT measured: see section 6.
4. **Type I at e = 0 is 0 of 3000 for every candidate (Wilson 95 % upper bound 0.0009, family level 0.0167).** The floor bound alone
   passes 0.4-1.4 % (the Wilson upper bound of 1m-metals ICT, 0.0176, is marginally above 0.0167; the conjunction is far below).
5. **Inclusion rule re-applied at the final parameters (section 4): 1m-metals INCLUDED (best ratio 0.59), 1m-indices INCLUDED (1.10),
   5m-metals NOT SATISFIED under the earlier primary convention (best ratio 2.06, ICT; k = 2).** It is INCLUDED at k = 2.5 and 3, and at
   k = 2 under the measured-rate and rho-0 conventions (1.68 / 1.52): the verdict for 5m-metals depends on the convention and sits on the
   rule's edge. **Flag for the owner: the cells file says 5m-metals was kept by "best ratio 1.26"; with 7 folds that no longer holds.**
   Together with point 3 (frequency cap) this cell is the weak one. The cells file was not edited.

## 2. What is real and what is a model

Real harness code, imported and called (never re-implemented): `scripts/fund_stats.py` `robust_lower_bound` / `check_lower_bound` (the five
bounds at 1 - 0.10/6 = 0.98333), `check_fold_sufficiency` (the `insufficient` guard: any fold < 30 trades), `check_stability` (>= ceil(2m/3)
symbols positive, no trade > 25 % of net R), `check_frequency`, `check_regime_split` (D1 ADX median split, both halves positive),
`check_perturbation` (one neighbour at a time), `check_stress` with `stressed_net_r` / `stress_commission_r`, `prop_shift_r`, `check_prop_pass`,
`check_prop_pass_shifted`, `verdict_from`, `evaluate_cell` (full mode), `make_folds`; `scripts/performance.py` `metrics` with prop-search's own
FUNDS / horizon / account profiles; `fund-search.py` `prop_row_from_metric`; `scripts/research/power_model.py` (`p_from_edge`, `seed_for`, `run_prop`
for e*, `wilson_upper`) and `dev_start_decision.py` (`folds_for`, `fold_rate`, `build_rates`). Two evaluation modes: LAZY (each check is the
harness function, cheap first, stop at the first failing check: the verdict of `verdict_from` is the same because one failing check fails the
conjunction) and FULL (`FS.evaluate_cell` with every check computed). `selfcheck` asserts they agree (24 + 24 streams at e in {0.2, 0.35, 0.5, 0.7} for
5m-metals Wyckoff and 1m-indices ICT: 24/24 and 24/24 identical verdicts) and that the vectorised stress repricing equals `fund_stats.stressed_net_r`.

Not run: the harness engine's `simulate()` with live-parity sizing (as in the earlier power model A8 and the tests' stub engine, the prop link reads net R and
entry date only); the nested walk-forward (the pooled test trades are drawn directly at the true edge, see E10).

## 3. Assumptions (every one is a model choice, not a measurement)


- E1 [measured counts, final starts] per-symbol yearly admitted-trade rates = the calendar-year counts of docs/audits/2026-10-02-dev-start-counts.jsonl (5m-metals, 1m-indices), pro rata per fold window (dev_start_decision.fold_rate); a symbol with no bars in a fold has rate 0 (availability-aware: XPT/XPD absent-ish before 2017, 1m-indices late symbols). 1m-metals has no per-year counts: b10's pooled rate (ICT 675.7, Wyckoff 448.7 /yr) split equally over the 2 symbols, constant over the 9 folds.
- E2 PRIMARY variant sum03 = the measured pooled rate (the sum over symbols: every admitted trade the harness would pool) with month-level regime correlation rho 0.3. The earlier rule's convention disc03 (rate x 1/(1+(k-1)*0.3) AND rho 0.3: the correlation is counted twice, conservative) is run in parallel (curves, e_min, e*), and is the variant the inclusion rule is re-applied under (it was the earlier PRIMARY); sum0 / disc06 are the earlier rho sensitivities, sum06 = measured rate with regime rho 0.6.
- E3 [model, as power_model A1-A5] cost c = 0.07R (sensitivity 0.10); binary +2.5R / -1R, net_R = outcome - c_i; p = (e + 1 + c)/3.5 so the mean net R is the true net edge e; month-level lognormal arrival multiplier sigma 0.75 (mean 1) and month-level win-probability shift tau 0.03 (sd, in p), cross-symbol correlation rho on both; weekday-only Poisson arrivals.
- E4 [declared shape, NOT measured] entry hour-of-day (UTC): weights 0.8 (00-05), 1.2 (06-07), 1.8 (08-11), 2.2 (12-16), 1.4 (17-20), 0.5 (21-23), minute/second uniform; same for every symbol. The hour only matters through the cost multipliers of E7 and the entry date/month blocks.
- E5 [declared, INDEPENDENT of outcome] D1 ADX covariate adx14_d1 = 10 + 40 U with U redrawn per (symbol, 20-day block): persistent within a block, independent of win/loss, so the regime split (<= pooled median vs > median, BOTH halves must have mean net R > 0) is a pure power cost (a real regime effect could only make it worse or better).
- E6 [declared, the MOST ARBITRARY part] perturbation neighbours: n_nb distinct (item, component, direction) sets (ICT 11, Wyckoff 5: chosen value interior on every ordinal component; fewer if a chosen value sits on an edge). Each neighbour pools the same trade entries as the chosen value (same timestamps, same costs) with edge e - delta_j, delta_j ~ U(0, delta_max) drawn per neighbour (base delta_max 0.05, sensitivity 0 / 0.10); each trade's outcome is SHARED with the chosen stream with probability kappa (base 0.7; sensitivity 1.0 / 0.0), else an independent redraw at the neighbour's p. A neighbour passes iff pooled mean > 0 AND its primary bound > 0 at the floor (fund_stats.check_perturbation).
- E7 [real spread statistics, declared anchor] stress repricing: per symbol the REAL by-UTC-hour median / p90 recorded spreads of data/history/costs/ftmo (real_costs.spec). Anchored so that the EXPECTED median spread cost is c: c_i = c * med(sym,h) / E_w[med(sym)], stress cost_i = c * p90(sym,h) / E_w[med(sym)] (hour h of the entry, both legs at the entry hour, swap 0 because flat_before_rollover is on), the representative stop distance dist_sym = rel_median_spread(sym) / c with rel_median_spread = overall median points * point / real_costs.price_ref(sym). Commission margin via fund_stats.stress_commission_r(entry, stop), stressed net R via fund_stats.stressed_net_r. Gate = mean > 0 (fund_stats.check_stress).
- E8 [as power_model A8, NOT the engine] prop_pass: performance.metrics(trades, account, horizon 120) per fund, net_R + entry_time only (no simulate()/live-parity sizing: the 2-consecutive-loss halving and any trade the engine would refuse are NOT modelled). Unshifted AND shifted by (pooled mean - primary bound) as in evaluate_with_engine; both funds >= 0.70 (+ the low-confidence rule of fund_stats._prop_row).
- E9 [as the harness] verdict = fund_stats.verdict_from: insufficient if any fold has < 30 trades; otherwise PASS iff all nine checks are ok. Replications: 200 per (candidate, e) on the primary grid (stop early at >= 98.5 % pass after >= 50), 60 on the disc03 grid, 40 on sensitivity points, 80 on attribution, 3000 at e = 0 (type I), 300 (800 for 5m-metals) per floor-only point of the inclusion rule. Monte-Carlo SE of a power estimate near 0.8: 0.028 (200), 0.052 (60), 0.063 (40). 'e80' = smallest grid e (step 0.05) with power >= 0.80, read from the estimated curve (no smoothing).
- E10 the nested walk-forward is NOT simulated: the pooled TEST trades of the candidate are drawn directly at the true edge e (a procedure that picks a good value in training and then tests it can only do WORSE than a fixed value at edge e, so these power figures are an upper bound on the sealed procedure; a selection-induced shrinkage is outside this model).


Inputs by cell (all from the repository, none read from trade outcomes): folds 9 / 2 / 7 from `FS.make_folds` on the final starts (1m-metals 2012-05-03 null start, 1m-indices
2020-03-01, 5m-metals 2015-01-07; asserted in the script); per-symbol per-fold admitted-trade counts for 5m-metals and 1m-indices from
`docs/audits/2026-10-02-dev-start-counts.jsonl` (pro rata by calendar year as in `dev_start_decision.fold_rate`); 1m-metals pooled 676 / 449 per year
(`2026-10-01-cell-selection-rates.json`, b10's 2021-2023 slice rates, assumed constant over all 9 folds: no per-year counts exist for that cell).
Floor confidence 1 - 0.10/6 = 0.98333 for every bound (the earlier work used the per-method N of 232 / 128).

## 4. The pre-stated inclusion rule, re-applied mechanically

Rule (docs/audits/2026-10-01-cell-selection.md section 1): a cell is INCLUDED iff `e_min <= 2 x e*` for at least one of ICT and Wyckoff, with `e_min` = the
first grid edge at which the harness lower-bound rule (the five bounds) has power >= 0.80, grid 0.025 steps from 0.05 to 0.40 then 0.50 / 0.60 / 0.80 / 1.00
(coarse points 0.05 steps, then the 0.025 midpoint below the first crossing), and `e*` = the smallest edge with `prop_pass_probability >= 0.70` on the binding
fund (`power_model.run_prop`, 9-step bisection, population stream). The ONLY changes to the inputs are the final ones: folds 9 / 2 / 7 (was 9 / 4 / 17), the
availability-aware per-fold counts of the final starts, and the floor confidence 0.98333 (was the per-method N-adjusted one). The primary pooling variant is
the earlier one (`disc03`: rate discounted by 1/(1+(k-1)*0.3), regime rho 0.3). The same rule is also applied to the measured-rate variant (`sum03`) and the
two earlier rho variants, and k in {1.5, 2, 2.5, 3} is shown, so the owner can see how robust the verdict is. The full-conjunction e80 of the same cells is in T8.

## 6. Limits, and what would change these numbers

1. **Selection is not simulated (E10).** The sealed procedure picks values on training folds and is scored on the next fold; the pooled test trades here come from a FIXED
   value at the true edge. A procedure that has to select cannot beat that, so every e80 above is a lower bound on what the real procedure needs.
2. **The frequency result for 5m-metals rests on an assumed month-level rate dispersion (sigma 0.75, inherited from the earlier power model) and on the generator's
   hour/weekday arrival model, neither of which is measured.** The check itself is deterministic given the trade list: the entry timestamps of the admitted trades per fold
   (counts and dates, no outcome) tell directly whether the 30-day gap rule is feasible. That measurement is the one that settles points 3 and 5 of the bottom line; it was
   not made here (the task excluded engine changes and real-data reads beyond counts).
3. **Prop link without the engine.** `simulate()` with live-parity sizing and the 2-consecutive-loss halving are not modelled (E8): trades the engine would refuse or shrink would
   lower the real prop pass, so the real e* and the shifted-prop threshold are, if anything, higher.
4. **Rates.** 1m-metals uses b10's 2021-2023 pooled rate for all 9 folds; older years may have fewer or more trades. The 5m-metals and 1m-indices rates are the measured counts of
   each fold's calendar years, with the first partial year of a symbol a lower bound.
5. **Binary +2.5R / -1R outcomes, independent covariate, hour profile and neighbour model are declared shapes (E3-E6).** The realised-R distribution of the real engine (time stops, partials)
   differs; the bounds depend on its variance.
6. **Resolution.** e80 is read on a 0.05 grid from estimates with SE 0.03-0.06; treat differences of one grid step as noise.
7. **Two stages are cheap proxies:** the floor-only e_min uses 300 replications (800 for 5m-metals), the disc03 curves 60, the sensitivities 40.
8. Runtime: 2345 s (39.1 min) with 4 processes; seeds are fixed (`power_model.seed_for("e2e", ...)`, BASE_SEED 20261001), so a re-run reproduces the numbers up to the early-stopping rule.
