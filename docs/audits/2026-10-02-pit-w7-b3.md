# Point-in-time evidence for the higher-timeframe features: W7, B3 `tfa_p5`, B-POOL (2026-10-02)

Branch `b24-pit-w7-b3` (from `windows-migration` 5b7c617). Tests and a research probe only; no engine file was changed, no leak was found.
Counts and pass/fail only: no R, expectancy or win rate is computed or printed by any of the new code.

## 1. Answer in six lines

1. **No look-ahead found** in W7 (`fx_w7_htf_target`), in the B3 bias read (`fx_b3 = tfa_p5`) or in B-POOL (`fx_b_pool = on`): in every dedicated test and every
   real-data run the output at decision time t was identical when every higher-timeframe bar with `availableTime > t` was deleted or replaced by an adversarial
   value, and equal to an explicit-prefix oracle.
2. **The tests can see a leak.** Every check has a positive control inside the same test (a deliberately leaky variant: HTF prefix one period too late for W7,
   HTF bars one period early for B3, decision window one bar too long for B-POOL) and the clean assertion must RAISE on it. The control fired in every
   configuration (section 4). This is what the red-team probe lacked for W7 and for B3 at 1m (docs/audits/2026-10-02-engine-realism-census.md, "Leakage probe (I9)").
3. **Rungs (read from the code, not assumed).** W7 reads `HTF_OF[tf]` (`scripts/backtest-methods.py:772`, `automation.next_rung`, ratio >= 4): **1m -> 5m, 5m -> 30m**
   (not 15m). B3 reads `TFA_P5_BIAS_TF` (`scripts/backtest-methods.py:843`): **1m -> 15m, 5m -> 1H**. B-POOL has **no higher-timeframe series at all**: it aggregates the
   decision series' own window (`scripts/ict-scan.py:362-391`), so its PIT surface is the window end and the day / session completion rule.
4. Dedicated tests: `scripts/tests/test_pit_w7_b3.py`, 17 tests, about 50 s. **>= 200 Phase-D legs per decision timeframe** (214 at 1m, 208 at 5m), each probed.
5. Real data (development period only, bars before 2024-03-01): `scripts/research/pit_w7_real.py`, **>= 300 Phase-D decisions per timeframe on XAUUSD and on one index
   symbol**, 0 violations, the control fired in every run (section 5).
6. What this does NOT prove is in section 6 (a PASS is "nothing found with these inputs and this sensitivity", not a proof of absence).

## 3. Method of the dedicated tests (`scripts/tests/test_pit_w7_b3.py`)

* **Synthetic series with planted structure.** W7: accumulation / distribution fixtures (the WY-4 Backup/LPS fixture of `test_audit_round3_wyckoff.py`, and its price-inverted
  twin for the short side) back to back with random scale and per-bar noise, on BOTH the decision series and its HTF rung, so Phase-D legs occur by the hundreds. The legs
  counted are the `_htf_wyckoff_target` calls that `_fires_from` itself makes for a Phase-D leg (spied), not a number the generator promises. B3: ICT-flavoured random walk
  (displacement candles leave FVGs), HTF = aggregation of the decision series. B-POOL: the same walk, long enough for day and session boundaries.
* **Server-time grid.** Bar-open labels come from a Mon-Fri grid in FTMO server time converted with `mt5_time.to_utc` (the repo's one conversion; providers.json
  `mt5_bridge_ftmo`, US-dates DST convention), across the DST change of 2024-03-10 and 2024-11-03 (and the UK change of 2024-03-31 for the London session). The labels are UTC, so DST
  only changes the length of the weekend gap (an hour shorter / longer): the tests check that gap is present and the decisions around it are unmoved; they do not (and cannot) test a timezone
  computation inside the engine, because the engine does none for these reads. B-POOL's sessions ARE DST-aware (`scripts/sessions.py` zones) and are exercised across the three changes.
* **Per decision time t** (W7: every Phase-D leg's decision time; B3: HTF bar boundaries, LTF bar closes, the bars around the DST weekend), the output with the FULL series must equal
  (a) the output with every HTF bar with `availableTime > t` DELETED, (b) with every such bar replaced by a huge spike up, a spike down (and, for the boundary runs, an
  alternating whipsaw), (c) an explicit-prefix ORACLE that filters bars by this file's own `open + period <= t` (never the engine's prefix code) and, for W7, runs the detector on exactly
  that prefix. W7's leg-level check also compares the whole Phase-D leg `_fires_from` builds (target, entry, stop, drop-or-keep), not only the target. B3 is checked at the gate
  (`htf_bias_gate`) and at the engine's own decision (`_ict_candidate`: setup + gate). B-POOL: the complete analysis dict at day / session boundary bars, with the future deleted or spiked, plus a check
  that every B-POOL level in `pools` is one the explicit knowable window supports.
* **Boundary.** Decision times exactly at `availableTime` of an HTF bar (the bar is INCLUDED), 1 s before (excluded) and 1 s after; `test_the_inclusive_boundary_is_pinned_exactly`
  asserts the prefix is bars[0..j] / bars[0..j-1] / bars[0..j] and that the target equals the one computed on exactly those slices (and that including bar j moved the target somewhere, so the check can tell
  include from exclude).
* **Broken HTF series.** `gappy` (3 % of bars missing, 1 % duplicated: the engine's fast path), `disordered` (adjacent rows swapped and one row stamped 400 days in the future in the middle of
  the history: availableTime not non-decreasing, so the per-call fallback `_htf_wyckoff_target_scan` runs).
* **Minimum counts are asserted** (legs, decisions, non-None targets, both answers of the B3 gate, setups reaching B3, B-POOL levels, control firings) so a vacuous run FAILS.

## 6. What is and is not proven

Proven (for the inputs exercised):

* On every synthetic and real series above, W7's target and the Phase-D leg built from it, the B3 gate and `_ict_candidate`, and B-POOL's analysis depend only on HTF / window bars with `availableTime <= t`; the boundary is inclusive (a bar with
  `availableTime == t` is read, one second earlier it is not); missing and duplicated HTF bars and (W7) out-of-order / future-stamped rows do not change that.
* W7's fast path (`_HtfSeries`, prefix-length memo) agrees with an explicit-prefix oracle at HTF bar boundaries (memo-order independence of that fast path is pinned separately by `test_w7_htf_cache`).
* The harness has power against the leak it was designed for: reading the still-forming HTF bar (one period early) is detected for W7 on 6-8 % of synthetic legs, 8-20 % of real decisions, and on essentially every B3 / B-POOL decision.

NOT proven:

* **Absence of leaks in general.** The mutation method detects dependence on HTF bars after t. A leak through another channel (the decision series' own future, costs, calendar, a context series other than the HTF rung or the B3 pair, a provider correction) is out of scope here; the engine-wide probe is `scripts/research/leakage_probe_fund.py`.
* **Sensitivity is limited for W7.** The control fires on 6-8 % of synthetic legs, 8 % to 20 % of real decisions (21 of 250 at US30 1m, 83 of 412 at XAUUSD 5m): most W7 decisions do not change when the forming HTF bar changes, so a leak that
  only moves a small fraction of decisions (below the control's own rate) could still hide inside 200-450 legs. Counts are reported so that rate is visible.
* **B3 at the engine level is thin**: 12 and 10 distinct ICT setups reached the gate (75 and 108 decision bars). The weight of the B3 evidence is the gate-level check (210-234 decisions per case, both answers well represented), which is
  the HTF read itself; `_ict_candidate` adds only the connection.
* **B-POOL at 1m is thin**: a 360-bar window (6 h) rarely holds a complete Asia / London run and never holds a previous trading day, so only 8-10 B-POOL levels were checked; the 5m rows (83-117 levels) carry the weight. B-POOL is a
  window-only feature; this test shows its window ends at bar i and its completion rule uses only knowable bars, not anything about a higher timeframe.
* **Unsorted HTF input to B3.** `htf_bias_gate` does a `bisect_right` over the HTF availableTime strings and so assumes a sorted series (history_store asserts ascending order across parts, `scripts/history_store.py` `_read_split`); the tests feed B3 missing and duplicated bars but not
  out-of-order ones (W7 handles those explicitly through its fallback path and is tested with them). Not a leak on valid data; unverified on invalid data.
* **Synthetic series are not markets.** They are built so Phase-D legs and ICT setups occur by the hundreds; the real-data probe (W7 only) covers the real structure, B3 and B-POOL have synthetic evidence plus the red-team probe's real-data PASSes.
* **Real runs are on the development period only** (before 2024-03-01), on year slices (the HTF prefix starts at the first loaded year), and 1m runs checked the first 350-400 of ~1,500 decisions found, with the control on the first 250.
* A PASS here is "nothing found with these inputs at this sensitivity". If the pre-registration needs a stronger statement for B3 at 1m or B-POOL, the next step is a larger real-data B3 probe with decision-anchored cuts (the red-team probe's method) at the sample sizes of section 5.
