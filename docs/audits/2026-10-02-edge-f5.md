- PGMs (XPTUSD, XPDUSD): G9 / H7 look strong in discovery (+22 / +26 bp) and collapse in confirmation and holdout (-30 to -72
  bp); spreads of 22 / 41 bp round trip dominate. E5 is negative everywhere on them.
- Indices (JP225, HK50, UK100, EU50, US2000, SPN35, N25): nothing passes BH-and-net; E5's two BH rejections (EU50, US2000) are
  net-negative. The Asian sessions do not carry the gold trend effect.

## What this settles

Across F2-F5 (153 pre-registered tests on 17 CFDs) the only robust intraday effects are: trend continuation on GOLD (H7, G9) and
SILVER (G9), and the E5 FVG retrace on XAUUSD / US500. They do not transfer to other indices or metals. The book cannot be made
materially faster at equal risk from this universe and these bar-based rule families
(docs/audits/2026-10-02-edge-f4.md §2). The baseline (docs/architecture/book-baseline.json) remains the book.

Remaining sources of a genuinely independent factor (owner decisions, not research choices):
1. Forex majors (FTMO's cheapest, most liquid class; a different return driver). Needs FX charts attached in MT5, a 5m history
   export, and the forex market re-declared in docs/architecture/instruments.json.
2. Nothing else here is a substitute: the forward PAPER + DEMO record of the baseline is the evidence that decides the goal.
