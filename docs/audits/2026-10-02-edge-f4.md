## 3. Conclusion

- The baseline stays the book. Adding G9 at 1 % buys speed (median 184 -> 92 d) by taking FAIL risk (0 -> 13 %); at equal
  daily risk it buys nothing. The owner's constraint ("rút ngắn thời gian mà không tăng risk") is NOT met by F4.
- G9 gold and silver go to the forward PAPER log (scripts/research/fvg_forward.py WATCH) for the stage-(a) read; not to the demo
  executor. XAGUSD paper needs the ExportOHLCV 5m chart attached for XAGUSD on the MT5 machine.
- The binding constraint is factor diversity, not the count of rules: a new component must come from a DIFFERENT return driver
  (other sessions / asset classes). Next family F5: transfer the three surviving rule types (H7, G9, E5) to the research-only
  symbols (JP225, HK50, UK100, EU50, US2000, SPN35, N25, XPTUSD, XPDUSD), pre-registered, same three reads. Any survivor there
  needs an owner allowlist decision before demo.
