## What the census does show (read with §5 of the pre-registration: none of this is a confirmed edge)

1. **There is real, replicating predictability -- smaller than the spread.** Several tests are significant in BOTH periods
   with the same sign (excess over the same-time-of-day placebo, CR1 by date):
   - E1 metals, sweep of the previous day's high/low that closes back inside: **continuation, not reversal** (direction -,
     i.e. the "stop-hunt reversal" reading loses): excess z 0.08-0.10 in discovery, 0.10-0.14 in confirmation, p < 1e-4 in
     both. Net in confirmation +2.1 bp (h = 12) and +4.6 bp (h = 24); negative under the p90 spread.
   - E2 metals, first close beyond the previous day's range: **fade** (direction -): significant in both periods, net ~0.
   - E5 FVG retrace (the ICT entry primitive), continuation: significant in both groups and both periods (excess z
     0.03-0.05), but net negative everywhere (-0.3 to -1.8 bp): the move after the retrace is a fraction of the spread.
2. **Cost is the binding constraint, and it is symbol-specific.** Median round-trip cost: XAUUSD 0.65 bp, US30 0.43, DE40 0.69,
   USTEC 0.77, US500 0.83 vs XAGUSD 4.37 bp. Pooling XAGUSD into "metals" sank the metals net figures: in discovery E1 h = 24
   nets +2.98 bp on XAUUSD vs +0.84 on XAGUSD; E5 h = 12 +0.25 vs -2.14. (A per-symbol reading is POST HOC; see "Next".)
3. **The published intraday baselines did not replicate here** on this short index history: ORB (E6) and intraday momentum
   (E7) are not significant in discovery and negative net in confirmation. Indices carry 1.5-2.5 years of dense data, so
   their power is low; "not found" is not "absent".
4. Big-bar continuation (E9) and the Asia-range events (E3/E4): no robust signal.

## Reading for the goal (FTMO with any validated method)

- The census rules out the cheap hypothesis that a single primitive with a time exit is a tradable intraday edge at 5m.
- It also shows WHERE a viable component could come from: (i) gold, where cost is ~0.65 bp, and (ii) holding longer than
  120 min, because the signed move grows roughly with sqrt(h) while the cost is paid once. The E1 metals excess grows from
  h = 6 to h = 24 in both periods; that is the direction a follow-up should test.
- The ICT reading is contradicted where it is testable: the sweep-and-reclaim of a prior-day level is followed by
  CONTINUATION on metals, not reversal.
