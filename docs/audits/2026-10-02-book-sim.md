## Reading

- For the first time in this repository there is a book whose in-sample FTMO profile is robust across regimes: H7 on gold
  drives it; it almost never fails (wide stops, small realised losses) and its drawback is TIME (median ~6-19 months).
- Speed is the open problem, not survival: the realised risk per trade is far below the 1 % ceiling because the stop is
  wide and rarely hit. More independent components (more trades a day) are the way to shorten it without raising risk.
- Everything here is in-sample design. The decision comes from the forward stage of each component.
