# Power model of the fund-search lower-bound rule (2026-10-01)

Status: MODEL ONLY. No market data, no R, no expectancy and no performance figure of any kind was read. The only real inputs are the
trade RATES of `docs/audits/2026-10-01-trade-rates.md` (counts), the owner's fixed spans, N and confidence rule, and the labelled
assumptions of section 8. Nothing here is a statement about any cell's true edge. Nothing is declared, evaluated or sealed; no harness,
engine or threshold file was edited; no pre-registered rule is loosened (options that would change the pre-registration are labelled
so in sections 7-8).

Branch `b12-power-model` (from `windows-migration` 6e14cc2). Script `scripts/research/power_model.py` (seed base 20261001, every job
seeded from crc32(label); a re-run of a spot-checked job reproduced its stored result exactly). Outputs, all generated by the script:
`docs/audits/2026-10-01-power-model.json` (raw), `docs/audits/2026-10-01-power-model-tables.md` (all tables R0-R6; this note quotes
from it). Re-run: `python3 scripts/research/power_model.py --out /tmp/power.json --report /tmp/power_tables.md` (about 17 min on 4
processes; `--quick` is a smoke test only). Provenance of the committed JSON: one full run (1026 s) whose `prop` section was then
replaced by an `--only prop --merge-into` run (11 s) because the stream length of the prop link was revised after the full run
(`meta.merged_runs`); the code and seeds are the committed ones, a clean full re-run is expected to reproduce it (not re-run, to stay
inside the time budget).

## 1. What is simulated

- **Rule under test**: the harness's own `robust_lower_bound` is imported and called on synthetic trade lists, then the pass test of
  `check_lower_bound` (`value is not None and value > 0`) is applied. Functions used (file:line, `scripts/fund_stats.py`):
  `robust_lower_bound` :224 = min(`lower_bound` :163 iid Student-t, `_block_bound` :182 by `_date_key` :206, `_window_key` :210 (30-day),
  `_quarter_key` :214, `_half_key` :219), `t_quantile` :130, `n_adjusted_confidence` :152 (1 - 0.10/N), `check_lower_bound` :497,
  `MIN_FOLD_TRADES` :44. Nothing is re-implemented. The only wrapper is a pure memo of `fund_stats.ts` (ISO string to datetime) for speed;
  `selfcheck()` asserts the bound is bit-identical with and without it (`meta.selfcheck.memo_equal = true`).
- **Trades**: Poisson arrivals per UTC weekday over F 365-day folds ending 2024-03-01 (`DEV_CUTOFF`, so calendar quarters and
  half-years fall exactly as in the harness), expected pooled count = measured rate x F. Binary outcome +2.5R with probability p, -1R
  otherwise, minus a cost c; the NET edge is e = 3.5p - 1 - c, so p = (e + 1 + c)/3.5. Clustering: month-level lognormal rate multiplier
  (sigma) and a month-level regime shift of p (tau); both share a cross-symbol component rho.
- **Spans used** (owner, with the coordinator updates of 2026-10-01: all six cells keep their original data start): 1m-metals 9 folds,
  1m-indices 4, 5m-metals 17, 5m-indices 4, 15m-metals 17, 15m-indices 4 (the 4 / 2 / 14 of the dispatch were withdrawn). N: ICT 174
  (confidence 0.999425), Wyckoff 96 (0.998958).
- **Replications**: 200 per (method, cell, e) on the main grid (Monte-Carlo SE of a power estimate <= 0.035), 100 on sensitivity and
  option grids (SE <= 0.05). e_min is the first grid value whose estimate is >= 0.80, so it carries about one grid step of noise.

## 2. Why power is low where it is (R0, the critical values the rule applies)

Findings:
- The 120-day horizon is 120 resampled ACTIVE days (`_bootstrap_days`, performance.py:320-380: blocks hold only days that had a trade). A cell
  with 35 trades a year therefore gets about 130 trades in its "120 days"; time is not penalised. This is a property of the existing engine, not of
  this model; flagged, not changed.
- The hypothesis "higher frequency needs a smaller per-trade edge" holds only up to about 2 trades per active day: the 5m and 15m cells (1.1-1.9 trades per
  active day) and Wyckoff 1m-metals (2.4) need a binding e* of 0.09-0.14R. Beyond that the needed edge RISES: ICT 1m-metals (3.1 trades per active day) 0.213R, 1m-indices
  (4.7-7.2) 0.24-0.26R, because 1 % risk on every one of several same-day trades makes the 5 % daily-loss and 10 % drawdown rules bind.
- Prop-relevant e per cell = the binding (max over funds) e*: ICT 15m-indices 0.094, 15m-metals 0.139, 1m-indices 0.242, 1m-metals 0.213, 5m-indices 0.121,
  5m-metals 0.098; Wyckoff 0.100, 0.119, 0.256, 0.125, 0.135, 0.125 (same cell order). A single owner e of 0.20R is above e* for every cell except the
  1m-indices pair and ICT 1m-metals.
- Comparison with section 4: for the 5m-indices and both 15m cells e_min (0.30-0.60) is 2.5 to 6 times e*: a cell could pass a prop challenge at
  an edge the lower-bound rule cannot certify, so the pre-registered statistical bar, not the prop rules, is the binding constraint there. For
  the 1m cells e* (0.21-0.26 for three of four) is at or above e_min (0.15-0.20): the prop rules bind, not the statistics.

### 7c. Longer history (R5d). Folds are 365 days; the last test fold ends at the 2024-03-01 development cut-off.

## 9. Assumptions (all of them; none is a measurement)

- A1 cost c = 0.07R per trade (sensitivity 0.05 / 0.10). e is the NET edge: net_R = (+2.5 | -1) - c, so p = (e + 1 + c)/3.5.
- A2 arrivals: Poisson per UTC weekday (weekends 0); the per-symbol daily rate is multiplied by a lognormal month multiplier exp(sigma*z - sigma^2/2) (mean 1), sigma = 0.75 (sensitivity 0 / 0.5 / 1.0). Intraday time uniform. No session structure.
- A3 regime-dependent outcome: the win probability of a trade in month m is p + tau*z_m (clipped to [0,1]), tau = 0.03 (sensitivity 0 / 0.06); mean p, hence the mean edge e, is unchanged. This, not the rate multiplier alone, is what the block bounds can penalise (rate variation with iid outcomes only costs them degrees of freedom).
- A4 cross-symbol correlation rho = 0.30 of the monthly regime z (z_sym = sqrt(rho) z_common + sqrt(1-rho) z_idio), applied to both the rate and the outcome shift (sensitivity 0 / 0.6). Symbols in a cell are assumed exchangeable (same per-symbol rate).
- A5 binary +2.5R / -1R outcomes: no time-stops, partials, breakevens or realised-R shrinkage; realised R of real trades differs.
- A6 trade rates are the 2021-2023 slice rates of docs/audits/2026-10-01-trade-rates.md (pooled ADMITTED trades/yr per cell), taken as the expected long-run rate over all F folds; the real rate over older history may differ (index S1 is a lower bound).
- A7 folds are 365-day blocks ending 2024-03-01, F per cell as fixed by the owner; pooled TEST trades = all trades in those F years.
- A8 prop link: pooled synthetic stream of 20 years (population answer, removes small-sample noise of the real F-year stream), performance.metrics(..., horizon=120) with each fund's own account profile (1% risk/trade via effective_risk_pct, 5% daily loss on day-start equity, 10% max DD, profit target 10%/8%, FTMO min 4 trading days, The5ers 3 profitable days >= 0.5%). The harness's live_parity_sizing changes only pnl/size of `taken` trades, not net_R; performance.metrics reads net_R only (_r, performance.py:142), so it is not reproducible nor needed here; the 2-consecutive-loss risk halving is therefore NOT modelled.
- A9 replications: 200 per (method, cell, e) on the main grid, 100 on sensitivity / option grids; Monte-Carlo SE of a power estimate <= 0.035 (0.05 at 100). e_min is 'the first grid value with estimated power >= 0.80'.

Not modelled: session / intraday structure, trade overlap and the one-position-per-symbol rule beyond what the measured admitted rate already embodies, time-varying cost,
fat-tailed or autocorrelated R, the 30-day max-gap rule, the stability / regime / perturbation / volume-kind / single-trade-share checks (any of which can fail a cell
independently of the lower bound), and the prop engine's loss-throttled risk halving. The model gives necessary, not sufficient, conditions for a PASS verdict.
