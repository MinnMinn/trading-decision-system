## 1. Verdict

1. **The research E5 detector has a look-ahead selection, and E5's measured edge is mostly that artefact.**
   `edge_census.ev_fvg` lists every gap that is EVER touched, in FORMATION order, then keeps the first per (server day,
   side) (`edge_census.py:263-281`, `_first_per_day` at `:162`). On a day with two same-side gaps where the later gap B is
   touched first and the earlier gap A is touched later, the research trades A and drops B. At the moment B fills, choosing
   A requires knowing that A will be touched later -- future information (CLAUDE.md §8). The dropped B trades are, by
   construction, the ones after which price kept retracing to A's edge: on the full XAUUSD history they average
   **-0.14 R** (1,135 trades), on US500 **-0.09 R** (197). The demo executor cannot do this: it rests a limit on every new
   gap and the first FILL cancels its siblings (`fvg_demo.py`, `done_keys`), i.e. it trades B.
2. Under a point-in-time selection, E5 has no edge on either symbol (both sides, R per trade):

   | E5 component | research (look-ahead), as in book_sim | first FILL per day/side (demo's rule) | first FORMED gap only [post hoc, also PIT] |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | XAUUSD 24 bars, 2004-26 | +0.0042 (10,337) | **-0.0123** (10,337) | -0.0117 (9,045) |
   | XAUUSD 24 bars, book span 2021-10 -> | +0.0106 (2,566) | **-0.0089** (2,566) | -0.0051 (2,268) |
   | US500 48 bars, 2021-26 | +0.0084 (2,556) | **+0.0013** (2,556) | +0.0012 (2,228) |

   (research fill rule, spread x1, in both PIT columns.)
3. **The fill-rule question itself (the pre-registered one) is second order.** Under the demo's selection, at spread x1:
   XAUUSD longs research +0.0102 / live +0.0104 / plus_spread +0.0094 R (span); US500 longs research +0.0087 / **live -0.0013**
   / plus_spread +0.0079 R. The live buy limit fills 99.9 % of the research longs (XAUUSD 5,173 of 5,176; US500 1,277 of
   1,278) -- a deep-enough retrace almost always follows a touch -- so E5 longs DO enter on the demo; on US500 the live
   order costs about one spread per trade. Shorts are unaffected by the fill rule and are the side where the look-ahead
   damage concentrates (XAUUSD shorts -0.028 R on the span under the demo's selection).

- `live` E5-long mean R <= 0 on the book span at sx = 1: **US500 yes (-0.0013)**, XAUUSD no (+0.0104).
- `plus_spread` beats `live` on the span AND the full history at sx = 1: **US500 yes**, XAUUSD no. By the rule, a v3 long

- Spread sensitivity (live, both sides, span): XAUUSD -0.003 / -0.009 / -0.022 R and US500 +0.003 / -0.004 / -0.014 R at
  x0.5 / x1 / x2. Even at half today's recorded spread E5 is ~0 under the demo's selection.

## 3. Reach of the defect

- Affected: every E5 measurement that goes through `ev_fvg` -- the census E5 rows, F2 stage (b) (the E5 "survivors"),
  `fvg_book_sim.py`, `book_sim.py`, `pass_policy.py` (all books containing E5), book-baseline.json, the v1 / v2 decisions'
  evidence, F3 H5 and F5 E5 (both null anyway), and the forward PAPER log and the stage-(a) verdict
  (`fvg_forward.py:190` detects with `ev_fvg`; a forward read would re-introduce the selection on forward bars).
- Not affected [post hoc check]: E1-E4 and E9 (`edge_census.py:190-236`, `:284-295`) append events in bar order with entry
  at the next bar, so their first-per-day pick is point-in-time. H7 and G9 pick the first close beyond a level (time order).
- The live demo executor is point-in-time (first fill wins); its E5 trades are the "demo selection" rows above.

## 4. What this does NOT show

- It does not show that FVG retraces carry no information: the census' excess-z significance was also computed on the
  look-ahead population, so it must be re-measured with a PIT selection before anything is said either way.
- All of it is on already-read data; it is a correction of a measurement, not new evidence of an edge or its absence.
- The spread model is one 2022-07..2026-09 recording per UTC hour (reframes R6e).

## 5. For the owner (decisions, not taken here)
