## R1. Objective: "P(pass) per attempt" -> "funded value minus fees, per month"

**Hidden assumption.** A failed challenge is treated like a capital loss: the pass-policy study made "first-attempt fail
<= 10 %" an admissibility rule (docs/plans/2026-10-02-pass-policy-preregistration.md §2.1), and the 1 % real-money ceiling
(CLAUDE.md §34) is applied unchanged to a challenge.

**Why it may be wrong.** In a challenge the trader's money at risk is the fee and the time; the balance is simulated (FTMO
describes every account, funded included, as a simulated account; the 2-Step 100k fee is EUR 540, refunded with the first
reward, split 80 % rising to 90 % -- third-party 2026 summaries, ftmo.com itself was not reachable from this machine:
verify before a paid challenge). The payoff is option-like. Worse for the current objective: **pass probability is
purchasable without edge.** Synthetic, edge = 0 (`barrier-reframes.json` grid, Sharpe 0):

| book | vol/yr | P(funded <= 87 weekdays) | 1st-attempt fail | with retry until day 87 | mean fees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| edge 0, const | 22 % | 0.19 | 0.53 | 0.23 | 1.7 |
| edge 0, const | 40 % | 0.14 | 0.86 | **0.57** | 4.5 |
| Sharpe 1.37, dd3 | 22 % | 0.38 | 0.04 | 0.38 | 1.0 |

So "P(funded within 4 months)" cannot be the objective: a retry-and-gamble policy at zero edge scores 0.57 on it (the
diagnosis §4.3 saw the same thing from the other side). What edge buys, and volatility does not, is the value of the
FUNDED account. Synthetic 12-month payouts of a funded account (`funded_value_12m`; monthly withdrawal of 80 % of profit,
balance reset, ends at the first breach; no costs), vol 18 %/yr under dd3: **17 % of the balance at Sharpe 1.37 (survives
12 months 0.91), 10 % at 0.68 (0.78), 6 % at 0 (0.60)**. Edge roughly triples the funded value; the zero-edge 6 % is the
option value of withdrawing gains while losses only end the account -- the reason a pass is not evidence of edge.

**What it changes.** (a) Score every policy on a frontier: P(funded by T), expected fees, and expected 12-month funded
payouts at the measured AND the halved edge. (b) Separate the CHALLENGE risk policy (failure costs a fee) from the FUNDED
policy (failure costs the payout stream). (c) The owner states an exchange rate: how many fees is one month of delay worth?
Nothing here changes the 1 % ceiling; whether it should differ between phases is an owner decision, not a research result.

## R2. "We have a Sharpe-1.37 book" -> "we have a gold-trend regime bet whose speed rides on its weakest component"

**Evidence.**
- Regime: funded <= 122 d by start year 2021 0.00 | 2022 0.12 | 2023 0.04 | 2024 0.21 | 2025 0.50 | 2026 0.40
  (pass-policy.md §3). The headline 0.36 is the 2024+ confirmation starts; bootstrap 0.22, half edge 0.13 (§1). XAUUSD E5
  alone passed 0.0-0.12 of starts in 2008-2016 (fvg-book-sim.md §3).
- Composition (book-variants.json `components`, span 2021-10-12 -> 2026-09, n x mean R): E5 XAUUSD 2558 x 0.0108 = 27.6 R,
  E5 US500 2547 x 0.0085 = 21.7 R, H7 652 x 0.0458 = 29.9 R, G9 gold 918 x 0.0264 = 24.2 R. **E5 = 62 % of base3's R and
  48 % of v2's.**
- E5's net edge is below one spread: XAUUSD +0.35 bp over 2004-26 and "~0 in 2008-2020" against a 0.65 bp round trip; US500
  +0.59 bp against 0.83 bp (fvg-book-sim.md §1, edge-census.md §2).
- **Backtest/live fill mismatch on E5 longs.** Research fills a long when the BID bar's low touches the edge
  (`scripts/research/edge_census.py:276`) and then charges a full spread (`:387`). The demo places a BUY LIMIT at the same
  bid-derived edge (`scripts/fvg_demo.py:387`, edge from `fvg_gap_at` via `new_gaps`, no spread adjustment); MT5 triggers a
  buy limit on the ASK, i.e. only when bid <= edge - spread. Live longs are therefore a different population (the shallow
  touch-and-go retraces -- the best continuation trades -- are missed; fills come one spread cheaper). The sign of the net
  effect is unknown; its size is about one spread, i.e. the whole E5 edge. Shorts are consistent (sell limit on the bid).

**Update after Test #1 (docs/audits/2026-10-03-e5-live-fill.md).** The fill rule turned out second order; the real defect
is the research E5 SELECTION: `ev_fvg` keeps the first FORMED gap among those ever touched, which on two-gap days needs
future information. Under the demo's point-in-time selection E5 has no edge (XAUUSD -0.012 R, US500 +0.001 R), and the v2
book as executed funds within 122 days ~0.07-0.10 of the time (confirmation 0.10, bootstrap 0.07, half edge 0.07, fails
11-15 %), not 0.36; without E5 (H7 + G9 gold) ~0.01-0.04.

**What it changes.** Before adding anything, re-simulate E5 longs under the live fill rule on the already-read history (a
design check of an existing component, minutes of compute; pre-registered in deliverable B). Report P(funded) across all
start years, not the latest regime. If E5 longs collapse, the honest book is H7 + G9 gold -- one factor -- and the realistic
P(funded <= 4 months) is well below 0.36.

## R3. "Forward demo is the primary evidence" -> "forward can only measure execution inside our horizon"

**Evidence.** Stage (a) of F2 (preregistration §4): one read at >= 100 events, one-sided p < 0.10, Holm over the survivors.
Power at the edge AS MEASURED (`barrier-reframes.json` `forward_power`, normal approximation on book-variants per-trade
mean/sd):

| component | P(PASS) at n = 100, alpha 0.10 | first Holm step (alpha 0.025) | trades for 80 % power | years at its frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H7 XAUUSD | 0.32 | 0.12 | 700 | 5.3 |
| G9 XAUUSD | 0.20 | 0.07 | 2,281 | 12.3 |
| E5 XAUUSD | 0.15 | 0.04 | 6,984 | 13.5 |
| E5 US500 | 0.16 | 0.05 | 5,309 | 10.3 |

The stage-(a) verdict, due in ~8-9 months, will FAIL a true component 68-96 % of the time, and a PASS is close to luck. The
owner decision "forward demo is the primary evidence" (owner-decisions 2026-10-02 item 3) cannot be met for edge.

**What it changes.** Re-scope the forward stage to what it CAN measure within weeks: fill rate, slippage vs the paper twin,
realised spread at fill hours, rollover handling (`fvg_demo.py status` already logs the paper/demo pairs). Pre-register
tolerances and a kill rule for catastrophic execution deviation. Edge belief must come from history + mechanism + regime
analysis; do not kill (or promote) a component on a stage-(a) read that cannot discriminate.

## R4. "Risk = 1 % per trade" -> "the barrier race is set by daily volatility and state; a rarely-hit stop controls neither"

**Evidence.** Stops are hit on 0.9-8 % of trades (book-variants `stop_share`: E5 US500 0.009, E5 gold 0.034, H7 0.069, G9
0.082); per-trade sd is 0.29-0.59 R; "the realised risk per trade is far below the 1 % ceiling because the stop is wide and
rarely hit" (book-sim.md, Reading). Book daily sd 0.71 R (base3) / 0.95 R (v2) per active server day at 1 % -> roughly
11-17 %/yr. Synthetic, Sharpe 1.37:

| vol/yr | P(funded <= 87 wd) const | fail const | P(funded <= 87 wd) dd3 | fail dd3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 % | 0.11 | 0.04 | 0.10 | 0.00 |
| 15 % | 0.24 | 0.11 | 0.21 | 0.00 |
| 18 % | 0.33 | 0.18 | 0.30 | 0.01 |
| 22 % | 0.43 | 0.28 | **0.38** | **0.04** |
| 26 % | 0.46 | 0.41 | 0.41 | 0.13 |

At the book's own Sharpe it sits below the speed-optimal volatility; **dd3 is what makes ~22 % affordable** (fail 4 % vs 28 %
without it). Same picture at half edge (Sharpe 0.68: dd3 0.12 at 15 % -> 0.25 at 22 %, fail 7 %).

Inside the 1 % rule there are exactly two ways to raise volatility without new edge: (i) a tighter protective stop (k x
sigma x sqrt(h) with k < 2 buys ~2/k more contracts at the same 1 % at the stop; with time exits the expected P&L per contract
is unchanged, so mean and sd both scale by ~2/k and only stop-out truncation costs Sharpe); (ii) more concurrent independent
positions (`max_portfolio_risk_pct` 0.05, risk-config.json). (i) is leverage in substance. The owner must say whether "1 %"
means "worst case per trade" (then (i) is inside the rule) or "realised volatility" (then it is not).

Deadline problems have a known optimal shape -- risk depends on the distance to the goal and the time left (Browne 1999,
"Reaching goals by a deadline", Adv. Appl. Prob. 31: 551-577) -- and FTMO adds a ruin barrier and a daily barrier. dd3 encodes
only ruin avoidance and was one of two throttles tried. A "taper within 2 % of the target" variant raises P(funded) a little
at constant vol (Sharpe 1.37, 26 %: 0.47 vs 0.46) but not under the fail constraint -- state-dependence must be designed with
the fail cost in view, not bolted on.

**What it changes.** Express risk as a target daily volatility per state (phase, distance to target and floor, today's loss
budget), hard-capped by 1 % at the stop; measure stop-width variants with the pass-policy machinery (pre-registered in B).

## R5. Independence: "more setups in one account" -> "a different DRIVER, and accounts as a unit of diversification"

**Evidence.** Every robust allowlist edge is metals trend (edge-f4.md §1, edge-f5.md "What this settles"); G9 gold has 0.19
daily correlation with the base and adds no Sharpe at equal risk (edge-f4.md §2); two accounts on the same book: 0.23 vs 0.21
(pass-policy.md §2). Synthetic two-stream result (`two_accounts`, Sharpe 1.37 per stream, rho = 0):

| per-stream vol | two accounts: P(>= 1 funded) | expected funded | one account carrying both: P(funded) | its fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 % | 0.21 | 0.22 | **0.43** | 0.11 |
| 18 % | 0.57 | **0.69** | 0.58 | 0.29 |

Below the speed-optimal volatility (where the book is today), a second independent driver belongs IN the account; once the
account is at its optimal volatility, the next driver belongs in a SECOND account. ADR 0010 (one account, one market, one
Trading System) already supports both shapes.

**Contrarian on forex.** Forex is not automatically independent of this book: XAUUSD is quoted in USD, so part of a gold trend
is a USD trend, and intraday trend rules on USD majors may load on the same factor. I could not measure it (no FX history in
the repo). If the owner re-opens FX, the first test is the daily correlation of each FX stream with the gold book, and
non-USD crosses should be on the list.

**What it changes.** Rank candidates by their correlation with the book's daily P&L and their frequency, not by their own
t-statistic; look first at drivers that should not care about gold's trend (index liquidity/inventory premia, calendar flows).

## R6. The research protocol is too strict where it matters and too loose where it matters

**Too strict.**
- (a) **Seasonal premia are invisible by construction.** Every family since the census tests the EXCESS over the same-minute,
  same-hold placebo (`edge_census.py:17-18`, `:394-408`; reused by F2-F5, AMD). Any unconditional time-of-day, session or
  calendar return is exactly what the placebo subtracts. Example never measurable here: the overnight drift of US index
  futures around the European open (Boyarchenko, Larsen, Whelan, RFS 36(9) 2023; NY Fed SR 917). Note the paper's own finding
  that the unconditional 02:00-03:00 ET window is NOT profitable after the bid-ask spread (Sharpe 1.1 gross -> -0.5 net;
  1:30-3:30 -> 0.3 net) while reversals after sell-offs are much stronger -- the conditional version is the candidate.
- (b) **"Not found" is reported as "dead".** edge-f4.md §1: "turn-of-month (G7) [is] dead after costs/swap". Its nets were
  POSITIVE in all three reads on US500 (+4.1 / +9.1 / +6.6 bp), US30 (+6.9 / +4.5 / +8.6) and USTEC (+4.6 / +11.3 / +10.1),
  with 46-140 events per read: underpowered, not dead. Same for F3's H2 daily shock reversal on the indices (positive nets in
  confirmation and exposed on US30, USTEC, US500; n = 17-54). **The point is the wording and the missing power statement,
  NOT that G7 or H2 is promising** (round A, conceded): picking them now BECAUSE their read nets were positive would be
  selection after the fact. They may be re-tested only under a new pre-registration with a calendar-appropriate null, on
  unread data, counted in the experiment budget.
- (c) **The decision unit is a per-test p-value**, while the goal is the book's P(funded). A frequent, low-correlation
  component with a modest edge can be worth more than a significant, correlated one (G9 gold: significant, zero added
  Sharpe at equal risk).

**Too loose.**
- (d) **Effective sample size.** E5 gold's 10,329 trades are mostly two profitable regimes (2004-07, 2021-26;
  fvg-book-sim.md §1). Day-clustered SEs treat months of one regime as independent evidence.
- (e) **Costs.** One 2022-07..2026-09 relative-spread snapshot prices every year since 2004 (`real_costs.py:45-57`). For a
  component whose net edge is under one spread (E5), cost-model error alone decides the sign.
- (f) **Limit fills on bid bars** (R2).

**What it changes.** Add a seasonal null (net > 0 and excess over the instrument's own uniform drift) for time/calendar
hypotheses; report power next to every null result; judge components by Delta P(funded) with a haircut; demand a fill/cost
sensitivity at least as large as the edge for any component whose net edge is below one spread.

## R7. "3-4 months" -> say what a month is worth

FTMO 2-Step has no time limit (ftmo-rules-audit.md §1). In replay the base book passes Phase 1 eventually 0.91-0.96 with 0-3 %
fail, median 184 d (book-sim.md). The 3-4-month target is the only reason risk must rise. The choice is: (a) 6-9 months near
zero fail with today's book (IF E5 survives R2), (b) ~4 months by raising volatility under dd3 (R4: Sharpe 1.37 -> ~0.38
at 22 %, fail ~4 %, synthetic), or (c) a second driver (R5). Each month a funded account is not running costs its expected
payouts; each extra attempt costs a fee. A first exchange rate from R1's numbers: at Sharpe 1.37, 18 % vol, dd3, a funded
100k account is worth ~17 %/yr ~ 1.4 % of the balance per month ~ **2.4 fees per month of delay** (EUR 540 ~ 0.6 % of 100k;
~1.4 fees per month at half edge). On that rate, paying one extra fee to be funded a month sooner is worth it -- IF the
edge is real; with no edge, neither speed nor patience is worth anything. The owner's own rate decides (a) vs (b).

## 8. Where I may be wrong (read before critiquing)

- The synthetic book is i.i.d. Normal. Real books have fat tails, volatility clustering and regime persistence, all of which
  LOWER the safe volatility; the optimal-vol numbers are upper bounds on the gain. The bootstrap of pass_policy.py is the
  right tool to confirm R4 on the real sequences.
- The power table uses the edge AS MEASURED on 2021-26 -- an optimistic edge. A smaller true edge makes R3 stronger.
- Fees, split and the simulated-account status are third-party summaries; ftmo.com was unreachable here.
- R2's fill mismatch is established in code; its P&L effect is not measured yet.
